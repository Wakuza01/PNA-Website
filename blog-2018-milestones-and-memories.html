Company Updates · December 2018

2018: Milestones and Memories

Home / News & Insights / Company Updates

2018 was a significant year for P&A Fabricators. New equipment came online, key contracts were renewed, our ISO 9001:2015 certification was successfully maintained, and the team grew to meet increasing demand. Here's a look back at the year's highlights.

New Equipment Investment

The most significant capital investment of 2018 was the addition of a new AMADA press brake — extending our bending capacity and reducing scheduling pressure on existing machines. The new machine handles sheet widths up to 3 metres and is fully CNC-controlled, with back-gauge positioning accurate to within fractions of a millimetre.

This investment directly benefits clients with long-run repeat orders: more available machine time means shorter lead times and more consistent scheduling.

ISO 9001:2015 Recertification

P&A successfully passed its 2018 ISO 9001:2015 surveillance audit with zero major non-conformances. This is the result of a quality management system that runs through every department — not just a document that sits on a shelf. Every operator understands their role in the quality chain, and every non-conformance is tracked and closed out through our corrective action system.

Team Growth

P&A added several new team members in 2018, including additional production operators, a quality inspector, and an estimator experienced in reading complex fabrication drawings. This growth reflects both increased order volumes and a deliberate strategy to build internal capacity rather than rely on subcontracting during peak periods.

Looking Ahead to 2019

2019 will see the launch of the P&A Sheet Metal Training Academy — South Africa's first QCTO and MERSETA accredited sheet metal fabrication training programme. Additional technology investments are planned, and we'll be actively expanding our client base in the mining, food processing, and engineering sectors.

Thank you to all of our clients, suppliers, and team members who made 2018 a strong year for P&A. We look forward to building on this foundation in the year ahead.
