The P&A Sheet Metal Training Academy

Home / News & Insights / Academy

P&A Fabricators is proud to announce the launch of South Africa's first QCTO and MERSETA accredited sheet metal fabrication training academy — a purpose-built programme designed to develop the next generation of skilled fabricators right here in KwaZulu-Natal.

South Africa faces a significant skills shortage in precision manufacturing. Experienced sheet metal fabricators — those who understand press brake tooling, laser kerf compensation, weld distortion, and powder coat preparation — are increasingly difficult to find. P&A's response is to build that capability ourselves.

What Makes the Academy Different

This isn't a classroom course. Trainees work on the actual shop floor, using the same AMADA CNC laser cutters and press brakes that our production team operates daily. Theory is integrated with hands-on production work — so graduates leave with documented, auditable hours on real equipment, producing real components to real tolerances.

The programme is accredited by:

  • QCTO — Quality Council for Trades and Occupations
  • MERSETA — Manufacturing, Engineering and Related Services Sector Education and Training Authority

Who Should Apply

The academy is open to candidates with a Grade 12 (Matric) qualification and a demonstrated interest in precision manufacturing. We actively recruit from communities surrounding our New Germany facility, and we prioritise candidates who show aptitude in mathematics and technical drawing.

The Broader Mission

P&A's investment in the academy isn't just about filling our own workshop. We believe that developing nationally recognised fabrication skills benefits the entire South African manufacturing sector. Graduates who go on to work elsewhere carry ISO-compliant habits, documented competencies, and a standard of workmanship the industry needs.

For more information on intake dates and application requirements, visit our Training Academy page or contact us directly.
