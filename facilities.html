Facilities

3,500sqm purpose-built manufacturing plant in New Germany, KZN — two fabrication bays, in-house powder coating facility, and a specialist project bay. All operating under a single ISO 9001:2015 quality system.

Our Plant

Our Factories

The main fabrication facility covers 3,500sqm across two bays — each 40m long × 7m wide, serviced by purpose-built overhead cranes with a 6m hook height and 5-tonne lift capacity. The in-house epoxy powder coating factory occupies a dedicated 1,000sqm bay with its own 5-tonne overhead crane and a purpose-built curing oven. A third double-volume bay of 700sqm handles specialist and short-run projects. All CNC equipment is networked through MIE Trak — every job tracked from material issue to dispatch.

Machinery & Handling

Material Handling

Material movement throughout the plant is handled by 3-tonne and 2-tonne Toyota forklifts. CNC machinery — including AMADA press brakes and laser cutting equipment — is maintained and updated on an ongoing basis to ensure dimensional accuracy and repeatability across every production run.

Logistics

Transport

P&A operates a Freightliner Cat 515 horse with a 15m low-bed trailer, a MAN rigid truck with a 6m drawbar trailer, and 5-tonne and 2-tonne Isuzu trucks for all delivery and logistics.
