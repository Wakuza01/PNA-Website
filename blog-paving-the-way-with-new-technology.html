Paving the Way with New Technology

Home / News & Insights / Technology

At P&A Fabricators, standing still has never been an option. Continuous investment in CNC technology, quality management software, and process automation ensures that our clients — engineers, procurement managers, and OEM manufacturers — consistently receive components that meet spec, on time.

The fabrication industry moves fast. Tolerances that were acceptable five years ago are now insufficient for modern engineering applications. CAD-to-component workflows have compressed timelines, and clients increasingly submit drawings as DXF, DWG, STEP, or IGES files rather than hand-written specifications. P&A has invested in the infrastructure to match.

AMADA CNC Laser Cutting

Our AMADA CNC laser cutters deliver clean, burr-free profiles with dimensional accuracy that makes secondary finishing unnecessary in most applications. Mild steel, stainless steel (304 and 316), and aluminium sheet — from 0.5mm through to 20mm — are all processed on the same machines, with material changeovers managed through our production scheduling system.

Laser-cut profiles are nested using AMADA's nesting software to optimise material yield. This directly benefits clients on volume jobs: less offcut waste means more competitive pricing without compromising quality.

AMADA CNC Press Brakes

Consistent, repeatable bending is critical for fabrications that must assemble accurately. Our AMADA press brakes use back-gauge CNC positioning to hold bend angles and flange lengths to tight tolerances across entire production runs. Where a job demands a specific bend radius or springback allowance, our engineering team confirms these parameters at quoting stage — not on the shop floor after the fact.

MIE Trak Pro — Job Traceability

Every job processed at P&A is tracked through MIE Trak Pro, our enterprise manufacturing resource planning (MRP) system. This means every operation — from material receipt through laser cutting, forming, welding, and powder coating — carries a job number, operator record, and quality checkpoint. Clients who require full traceability documentation receive it as a matter of course, not as an exception.

What This Means for Your Project

Technology investment at P&A translates directly to client value: faster quoting, faster production, fewer non-conformances, and a documented quality trail. If your project has tight tolerances, complex geometries, or a requirement for ISO 9001-compliant traceability, speak to our engineering team before you go to tender.
