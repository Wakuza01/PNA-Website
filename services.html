In Detail

Everything We Do

Turnkey fabrication solutions for aluminium, carbon steel and stainless steel products — here is what sits behind each service.

Design & Engineering

From design through to the finished product, one team stays with your job. We turn your concept or CAD files into 3D SolidWorks product manufacturing drawings so the part you approve is the part we build.

  • 3D SolidWorks product manufacturing drawings
  • Work direct from DXF, DWG, STEP or IGES files
  • Concept-to-drawing where no drawings exist yet
  • Full technical drawings and BOMs before production
  • DFM review and client sign-off before material is cut

CNC Services & Machining

CNC programmed straight from your CAD file, so profiles and tolerances carry through unchanged from the first part to the thousandth. We work in stainless steel, mild steel and aluminium — from large hoppers and tanks to high-volume production runs.

  • CNC laser cutting — including brass, copper and aluminium
  • CNC punching
  • Tube cutting and tube bending
  • CNC press brake bending — up to 4000mm long
  • Bending, rolling and forming
  • Machining and milling
  • Stainless steel, mild steel and aluminium, plate from 1mm to 20mm
  • Tight tolerances across high-volume repeat runs

Manufacturing & Welding

Coded and non-coded TIG, MIG, laser and robotic welding in aluminium, carbon steel and stainless steel — with every job tracked from enquiry to delivery in MIE Trak.

  • Coded and non-coded welding
  • TIG and MIG welding
  • Robotic welding
  • Laser welding
  • Welded ducting from 1mm to 10mm
  • Light gauge to heavy structural, SANS-compliant

Epoxy Powder Coating

Parts are pre-treated and phosphated, then coated in a fully automated Nordson ColorMax spraying system. Finishing is done in-house, so there is no outsourcing delay and one team is accountable from the first cut to the final coat.

  • In-house epoxy powder coating
  • Pre-treatment and phosphating
  • Fully automated Nordson ColorMax spraying system
  • Full RAL colour range
  • Corrosion-rated finish

Turnkey Fabrication Solutions

Hand us the whole job. We take it from design through fabrication, finishing and delivery under one quality system.

  • Aluminium, carbon steel and stainless steel products
  • One-off prototypes through to high-volume runs
  • OEM supply with scalability and flexibility
  • National and international supply capability
  • Weekly deliveries to JHB and Richards Bay
