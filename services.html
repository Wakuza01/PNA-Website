What We Do

Our Services

From drawing to dispatch — CNC laser cutting, AMADA press brake forming, MIG/TIG/arc welding, and in-house powder coating. One facility, one ISO 9001:2015 quality system, one point of contact for your entire project.

01 / 06 Design & Engineering
01

Design &
Engineering

Submit your CAD files (DXF, DWG, STEP, or IGES) or brief us from concept. Our in-house engineering team produces full technical drawings, BOMs, and a design-for-manufacture review — with client sign-off before a single piece of material is cut.

DXF / DWG / STEP / IGES 3D CAD modelling Technical drawings & BOMs DFM review Reverse engineering Material selection advice
02

Sheet Metal
Fabrication

CNC laser cutting and AMADA press brake forming across mild steel, stainless steel, and aluminium — 1mm to 20mm plate. Complex profiles, tight tolerances, and consistent results on high-volume repeat production.

CNC laser cutting Plasma cutting AMADA press brake bending Plate rolling Punching & nibbling Mild steel / stainless / aluminium
03

Manufacturing
& Welding

MIG, TIG, and arc welding from light gauge sheet to heavy structural assemblies. All procedures SANS-compliant. Certified welders with full production traceability through MIE Trak — every weld job is logged and auditable.

MIG welding TIG welding Arc welding Structural assembly SANS-compliant procedures Certified & auditable
04

CNC
Services

CNC laser profiling, turret punching, and press brake programming from your CAD file — CAD to finished component in one facility. Tight tolerances maintained across high-volume repeat runs with no dimensional drift.

CNC laser profiling CNC turret punch CNC press brake programming Tight tolerance work High-volume repeat runs CAD-to-component workflow
05

Epoxy Powder
Coating

In-house epoxy powder coating with pre-treatment and phosphating — full RAL colour range, corrosion-rated finish. No outsourcing means no delays, no quality handoffs, and single-point accountability from fabrication through to final coat.

Epoxy powder coating Pre-treatment & phosphating Full RAL colour range Corrosion-rated finish In-house — no third-party delays Industrial & architectural spec
06

Training
Academy

The P&A Sheet Metal Training Academy is QCTO and MERSETA accredited — South Africa's first. We develop the next generation of fabricators with hands-on, NQF-aligned qualifications.

QCTO accredited MERSETA accredited South Africa's first Formal apprenticeships NQF-aligned certification KZN Premier initiative
