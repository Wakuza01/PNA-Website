What We Manufacture

Capabilities

From precision conveyor components to heavy-duty concrete moulds — if it involves sheet metal, we make it.

Full Range

What We Make

P&A manufactures across a wide range of product types for South Africa's industrial, transport, mining, and infrastructure sectors.

Conveyor Components & Switchboards

Precision conveyor components including frames, guards, chutes, and electrical switchboard enclosures for mining and industrial applications.

Generator Housings & Enclosures

Custom generator housings, acoustic enclosures, and electrical assemblies for standby and prime power applications across all generator sizes.

Truck & Bus Industry Components

Engine compartment covers, gearbox mounting components, body panels, and structural assemblies for the commercial vehicle industry including MAN and Bell.

Light to Heavy Duty Safes

Precision fabricated safes ranging from light-duty cash boxes to heavy-duty vault doors and commercial security enclosures for the banking and retail sector.

Boom & Turnstile Housings

Enclosures and housings for access control systems including boom gates, turnstiles, and pedestrian barriers for commercial and industrial sites.

Rail Components

Interior and structural components for rail coaches and locomotives. P&A has supplied the South African rail sector with precision fabricated components for decades.

Brackets for Any Application

Custom brackets, mounts, and support structures for any application — from light-duty instrument brackets to heavy structural mounting frames.

Hoppers, Tanks & Chutes

Custom hoppers, storage tanks, chutes, and transition pieces for bulk material handling in mining, grain, and industrial processing applications.

Cyclones for Particle Separation

Industrial cyclone separators for dust collection, particle separation, and air cleaning systems — fabricated in mild steel or stainless steel to specification.

Structural Steel Supports

Fabricated structural steel supports, frames, platforms, and gantries for industrial plant, equipment mounting, and construction applications.

Screw Conveyors & Bucket Elevators

Complete fabricated screw conveyors and bucket elevator systems for bulk material transport in grain, mining, and industrial processing facilities.

Welded Ducting (1mm to 10mm)

Precision welded rectangular and circular ducting systems from 1mm to 10mm sheet thickness, for HVAC, dust extraction, and industrial ventilation.

Concrete Moulds (Standard & Custom)

High-precision steel concrete moulds for precast concrete products — standard sizes and fully custom configurations for any precast application.

Non-Pressurised Vessels

Storage and process vessels for non-pressurised applications — water, chemicals, and bulk materials in mild steel, stainless steel, or aluminium.

Bag & Cartridge Filter Housings

Bag filter housings and cartridge filter housings in any size for industrial air filtration, dust collection, and process filtration applications.

Air Filtration & Deep Bed Scrubber Housings

Enclosures and housings for air filtration systems, scrubbers, and wet arrestors for industrial emission control and air quality management.
