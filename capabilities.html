Full Range

What We Make

P&A manufactures and fabricates a wide range of product types for South Africa's industrial, transport, mining, and infrastructure sectors.

Precision Conveyor Components

Precision conveyor components including frames, guards, and chutes, fabricated for mining and industrial applications.

Switchboard Components

Precision steel components for electrical switchboards, fabricated and finished to your drawings.

Sound & Weather Proof Generator Housings

Sound and weather proof generator housings, enclosures and assemblies for standby and prime power applications across all generator sizes.

Truck & Bus Components

Engine, gearbox and chassis mounting components for the truck and bus industry — a complete OEM production solution including engine compartment covers, body panels and structural assemblies for clients including Everstar, Bell and MAN.

Electronic Housings

Electronic housings fabricated in any size range, to your drawings.

Safes, Coin Acceptance & Dispensing Housings

Light to heavy duty safes, from cash boxes to vault doors, plus coin acceptance and dispensing housings and commercial security enclosures for the banking and retail sector.

Boom & Turnstile Housings

Boom and turnstile housings for any application, including access control systems, pedestrian barriers, and commercial and industrial sites.

Rail Components

Components for coaches and locomotives, for international and local supply. P&A has supplied the South African rail sector with precision fabricated components for decades.

Brackets for Any Application

Brackets for any application, any size or material — from light-duty instrument brackets to heavy structural mounting frames.

Hoppers, Tanks & Chutes

Custom hoppers, storage tanks, chutes, and transition pieces for bulk material handling in mining, grain, and industrial processing applications.

Cyclones for Particle Separation

Industrial cyclone separators for dust collection, particle separation, and air cleaning systems — fabricated in mild steel or stainless steel to specification.

Structural Steel Supports

Fabricated structural steel supports, frames, platforms, and gantries for industrial plant, equipment mounting, and construction applications.

Screw Conveyors & Bucket Elevators

Complete fabricated screw conveyors and bucket elevator systems for bulk material transport in grain, mining, and industrial processing facilities.

Welded Ducting (1mm to 10mm)

Precision welded rectangular and circular ducting systems from 1mm to 10mm sheet thickness, for HVAC, dust extraction, and industrial ventilation.

Concrete Moulds (Standard & Custom)

High-precision steel concrete moulds, standard and specials, for precast products such as roadside curbs, barriers and lintels.

Non-Pressurised Vessels

Storage and process vessels for non-pressurised applications — water, chemicals, and bulk materials in mild steel, stainless steel, or aluminium.

Bag & Cartridge Filter Housings

Bag filter housings and cartridge filter housings in any size for industrial air filtration, dust collection, and process filtration applications.

Air Filtration & Deep Bed Scrubber Housings

Enclosures and housings for air filtration systems, scrubbers, and wet arrestors for industrial emission control and air quality management.
