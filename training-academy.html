Our Academy

Built on a Vision

The P&A Sheet Metal Training Academy was founded on the conviction that South Africa's manufacturing sector needs skilled, work-ready fabricators — and that P&A had a responsibility to help produce them.

Under the leadership of Managing Director Gerald Anthony, P&A invested in creating a structured, accredited training environment within the production facility itself. Learners train alongside experienced artisans, working on real components for real clients — not just simulations.

The Academy achieved QCTO and MERSETA accreditation, making it the first of its kind in South Africa. Every graduate leaves with a nationally recognised qualification in sheet metal work.

Government Partnership

KZN Premier Office Initiative

P&A partnered with the KwaZulu-Natal Premier's Office to provide structured apprenticeship training for 50 learners as part of a provincial skills development initiative. This programme gave disadvantaged youth in KZN access to a structured, accredited trade qualification in sheet metal fabrication.

Learners received both theoretical instruction and hands-on practical training, progressing through the fabrication process from raw material handling and safety, through to completed component production. The majority of graduates went on to employment in the manufacturing sector.

This initiative reflects P&A's broader commitment to skills development, transformation, and the sustainable growth of South Africa's manufacturing industry.

Programme Information

What You Will Learn

Our accredited programme covers the full range of sheet metal fabrication skills, aligned with the South African National Qualifications Framework.

Health & Safety

Workplace safety procedures, PPE usage, hazard identification, and emergency response protocols for the fabrication environment.

Technical Drawing Reading

Interpreting engineering drawings, tolerances, material specifications, and symbols used in sheet metal fabrication production.

Marking Out & Cutting

Accurate marking out of sheet metal, safe use of hand tools, and operation of cutting equipment including guillotines and plasma cutters.

Bending & Forming

Operating press brakes and folding machines, understanding bend allowances, and producing accurate formed components from flat sheet.

Welding Fundamentals

Introduction to MIG and TIG welding processes, joint preparation, weld quality standards, and basic welding practice on sheet metal.

Quality Control & Inspection

Measuring and inspection techniques, quality standards, defect identification, and documentation within an ISO 9001 quality system.
