Company Updates · November 2018

48 Years in the Industry

Home / News & Insights / Company Updates

In 2019, P&A Fabricators celebrated 48 years in the sheet metal fabrication industry. Founded in 1971, the company has grown from a small KwaZulu-Natal workshop into one of South Africa's most trusted precision fabricators — serving mining, automotive, food processing, and general engineering sectors across the country.

What started as a modest operation has evolved considerably — in scale, technology, and capability. But some things haven't changed: a commitment to quality craftsmanship, an expectation that components will meet drawing specification, and a culture that takes pride in the work leaving the building.

From Manual to CNC

The shift from manual press brakes and shears to CNC laser cutting and computer-controlled bending was the single biggest transformation in P&A's history. What once took skilled operators hours of setup and manual measurement can now be programmed from a DXF file and run repeatably across hundreds of parts without dimensional drift.

The engineering team has grown alongside the machinery. Today, P&A's estimators and production planners read CAD files, understand GD&T tolerancing, and know the difference between a bend allowance calculated for mild steel and one calculated for stainless.

ISO 9001:2015 — Quality as a System

P&A achieved ISO 9001 certification and has maintained it through every subsequent revision of the standard. For engineers sourcing fabrication, ISO certification isn't just a badge — it's evidence of documented procedures, calibrated equipment, non-conformance tracking, and internal audits. It means that when P&A says a component has been checked, there's a record to prove it.

Looking Forward

As P&A moves toward its sixth decade, the focus remains on the same fundamentals: capable plant, skilled people, and a quality system that doesn't drift. New technology will continue to be adopted where it demonstrably improves output — but not for its own sake.

If you're an engineer or procurement manager looking for a fabricator with genuine depth of experience, we'd welcome the conversation.
