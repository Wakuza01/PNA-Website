Our Story

50 Years of Sheet Metal. Built to Engineering Spec.

P&A is a proudly South African sheet metal fabricating and manufacturing company with over 50 years of experience. We work directly from your DXF, DWG, or STEP files — and where drawings don't exist yet, our in-house engineering team will produce them. Clients include MAN, Bell Equipment, Cash Connect Group, ND Engineering, and Bosch Projects.

Founded in 1971 and operating under ISO 9001:2015, every job is tracked from enquiry to delivery through MIE Trak — giving you full production visibility, documented quality control, and reliable scheduling on every order.

MIE Trak — Advanced Manufacturing Tracking

Learn More

In 2007, P&A became the first company in Africa to implement MIE Trak — an advanced electronic manufacturing tracking system that monitors every job from enquiry through to delivery, including a full bill of materials.

Every job receives a unique tracking number. At any stage, you can know exactly where your components are in the production cycle — from material issue through to final inspection and dispatch.
