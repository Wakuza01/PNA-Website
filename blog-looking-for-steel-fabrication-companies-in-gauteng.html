Looking for Steel Fabrication Companies in Gauteng?

Home / News & Insights / Industry News

If you're searching for steel fabrication companies in Gauteng, you may be surprised to find that one of South Africa's most capable sheet metal fabricators is based right here in KwaZulu-Natal — and we deliver to Gauteng and every other province, nationwide.

It's a common assumption: if you're in Johannesburg or Pretoria, you need a fabricator on your doorstep. But in today's logistics environment, proximity matters far less than capability, quality, and reliability. P&A Fabricators has been supplying clients across South Africa — including major industrial operations in Gauteng — since 1971.

What Gauteng Clients Need from a Fabricator

Engineers and procurement teams in Gauteng typically require:

  • Consistent dimensional accuracy across high-volume runs
  • Full material traceability and quality documentation
  • The ability to work from DXF, DWG, STEP, or IGES files
  • ISO 9001:2015 certification
  • In-house finishing (powder coating, welding) to reduce supply chain risk

P&A meets all of these requirements — and does so from a fully equipped 3,500 m² facility in New Germany, KwaZulu-Natal.

Our Capabilities at a Glance

Our facility is equipped with AMADA CNC laser cutting machines, AMADA CNC press brakes, and a full MIG/TIG/arc welding bay. All jobs are tracked through MIE Trak Pro, giving clients full job-number traceability from enquiry through to delivery.

We process mild steel, stainless steel (304 and 316), and aluminium — in gauges from 0.5mm sheet through to 20mm plate. Tolerances of ±0.5mm are standard; tighter tolerances are available on request and confirmed at quoting stage.

Delivering to Gauteng

We use reliable third-party logistics partners for Gauteng deliveries, with lead times quoted on a per-job basis depending on complexity and volume. For large ongoing contracts, scheduled delivery runs can be arranged.

If you've been settling for a local fabricator because you assumed distance was a barrier — it isn't. Contact us with your drawings and let's talk spec.
